# China Career Coverage Matrix v1

**Status:** current planning source for China-oriented medical-background
career coverage. This matrix recommends product coverage paths; it does not
create a Role Pack, routing target, or claim that any listed role is currently
available without the applicable runtime support.

## Method and limits

The matrix synthesizes the completed career-coverage research into stable
responsibilities, ownership boundaries, and an appropriate product tier. Its
`high`, `medium`, and `low` demand signals are transparent planning heuristics
for likely frequency among medical-background career changers in China; they
are **not** labor-market share, vacancy volume, salary, or hiring-probability
statistics. Evidence counts are qualitative: `established` means an existing
canonical Pack; `multi-JD` means multiple reviewed employer JD signals; and
`scoping` means the direction needs a dedicated China JD corpus before a
graduation decision.

## Coverage interpretation

- **Deep / Canonical now:** seven canonical directions: Academic / PhD, Clinical
  Research, Medical Affairs / MSL, Health Data / AI, Clinical Operations, CRA
  support, and CDM support. Canonical status comes from `data/role-packs/*.json`;
  it does not by itself promise a routing target.
- **Promotion / Candidate path:** medical-device clinical/application work is
  in a separately scoped Canonical promotion pipeline; PV remains a dedicated
  Candidate corpus. Neither is canonical until a promotion PR, required tests,
  and traceable human approval exist.
- **Research / Beta path:** Medical Writing, Regulatory Affairs, RWE, and HEOR
  remain planning or research directions unless a dedicated Candidate corpus is
  recorded. They must not be presented as executable Packs merely because they
  appear in this matrix.
- **JD-driven path:** Market Access, Product, Consulting, Commercial Analytics,
  Healthcare/Project Operations, and Medical Sales should retain a concrete JD
  and explicit gap/ownership mapping rather than be forced into a universal
  Pack.

The planning estimate is **75–85% usable coverage** of common
medical-background transition demand only when the seven current Canonical
directions are presented alongside the Generalist JD-driven route and bounded,
JD-required Candidate/research paths. It is a heuristic range, not a measured
market-share result. Canonical-only coverage is materially lower and must not
be advertised as this range.
