# China Clinical Data Management Candidate scorecard

**Current tier:** Beta research. **Release Gate:** `FAIL — remain Candidate/Beta`.

The evidence contains two full China source records, one partial record, and one
non-countable search extract; it does not represent graduation-level JD
coverage. The stable core is trial-data review/cleaning support, controlled
data-management documentation, bounded EDC/CRF validation support, and
GCP/SOP-aligned data quality.

Hard gaps include database-lock and EDC-build ownership, budget/client delivery,
people management, and tool-specific expertise. The fixed eight-persona set,
direct/transferable/partial/gap mappings, machine-readable negative mappings,
and three evaluation cases are present. Factuality, ownership, usefulness, and
critical unsupported claims remain unscored because no generated outputs have
been audited. Full snapshots, audit evidence, and conformance provenance remain
required before Candidate graduation or canonicalization review.
