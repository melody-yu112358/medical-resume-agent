## Candidate assets and boundary status

The stable core is bounded trial-data checking/cleaning/query follow-up,
controlled documentation, assigned-scope CRF/EDC/UAT support, GCP/SOP quality
and confidentiality, and scoped coordination. Eight fixed personas each map to
three qualifying JDs. Direct, transferable, partial, and gap mappings; five
machine-readable negative rules; and positive/partial/negative evaluation cases
are present.

Hard gaps remain direct proof of database-lock ownership, independent EDC build,
end-to-end delivery, budget/client/vendor ownership, people management, and
tool-specific expertise. The negative rules prohibit upgrading cleaning or
checking to ownership, generic analysis to clinical-data-management experience,
communication to project/client ownership, filing to CRF/EDC authority, and
absent tools/workflows to CDISC, coding, budget, or team-management claims.
