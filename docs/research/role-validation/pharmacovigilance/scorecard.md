# China Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Candidate scorecard

**Current tier:** Beta research. **Release Gate:** `FAIL — remain Candidate/Beta`.

Only one full verified China source record is available. One official source is
partial, one third-party listing is explicitly unverified, and two are
non-countable search extracts. The provisional stable core is regulated
safety-case and documentation support, GVP/SOP-aligned quality support, and
bounded safety-information coordination—not safety strategy or medical judgment.

Hard gaps include ICSR submission ownership, signal/benefit-risk work,
PSMF/QPPV responsibility, regulatory communication, risk-management ownership,
and people management. Eight fixed personas, mappings, machine-readable negative
mappings, and positive/partial/negative cases are included. Factuality,
ownership, usefulness, and critical unsupported claims are unscored until
audited generated outputs exist. Verified full snapshots, domain review, and
conformance provenance are required before Candidate graduation.
