## Stable core and boundaries

The recurring, entry-to-specialist core is regulated safety-information intake,
processing and follow-up; controlled safety documentation, reconciliation and
quality support; GVP/SOP-aligned record and inspection-readiness support; and
bounded coordination of assigned safety information. It does **not** establish
personal evidence for ICSR submission ownership, medical review, signal or
benefit-risk ownership, PSMF/QPPV responsibility, regulatory representation,
PV-system ownership, or people management.

The existing machine-readable negative mappings remain complete. Senior and
physician source records are retained to distinguish career levels, not to
broaden junior/support persona claims.
