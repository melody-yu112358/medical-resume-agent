# Medical capability taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to make a resume accurate and readable. It is a writing aid, not a checklist of skills the user is assumed to have.

| Category | What belongs here | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research design and methods | How a research question was designed or analysed | cohort study, RCT, Meta-analysis, MR, GWAS, bioinformatics, machine learning |
| Data and technical tools | Languages, software and concrete data work | R, Python, SPSS, SQL, data cleaning, statistical analysis, visualization |
| Clinical research design and execution | Protocol and study-operation work | eligibility criteria, follow-up, CRF, ethics, GCP, real-world study, data quality control |
| Experimental techniques | Laboratory methods and experimental systems | cell culture, qPCR, Western Blot, flow cytometry, ELISA, animal experiments, tissue staining |
| Medical evidence and information | Evidence retrieval, appraisal and medical communication | PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, guideline interpretation, evidence grading, medical writing, medical communication |
| Role and deliverable | What the user personally owned or delivered | screening records, analysis script, figure, presentation, research report, manuscript contribution |

## Important distinctions

- **R/Python/SPSS/SQL** are tools, not research methods.
- **MR, Meta-analysis, cohort study and RCT** are study designs or analytical methods.
- **qPCR, Western Blot, flow cytometry and ELISA** are experimental techniques.
- **PubMed, Embase and Cochrane** are evidence-retrieval resources, not a generic technical stack.
- **GCP and ethics** describe research standards or compliance knowledge.
- **CRF** is a study document or workflow artifact; mention it only when the person actually designed, completed, reviewed, or maintained it.

## Resume presentation rules

1. Put a capability in the experience where it was used whenever possible; do not list it as an isolated keyword.
2. Separate methods, tools and techniques in any skill summary. A concise grouping is better than a long mixed list.
3. A term belongs in the final resume only if the user can explain their role and the setting in which it was used.
4. For entry-level users, “assisted with” or “completed under supervision” can be more credible than an unqualified ownership claim.
